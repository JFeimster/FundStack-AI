# Content Style Guide

This guide defines the voice, messaging style, and conversion posture for FundStack AI public pages, partner assets, tools, and internal documentation.

## Brand Positioning

FundStack AI is a practical fintech operating layer for funding partners and business owners. It should feel like a sharp, modern command center — not a bank brochure, not a SaaS cliché machine, and not a motivational poster wearing loafers.

The site should communicate:

- Practical funding intelligence.
- Partner enablement.
- AI-assisted triage.
- Business-credit and funding readiness.
- White-labeled operating discipline.
- Speed without reckless guarantees.

## Core Voice

The FundStack / Moonshine voice is:

- Direct.
- Strategic.
- High-signal.
- Partner-first.
- Operator-friendly.
- Clear over cute.
- Bold without making illegal promises.
- Modern fintech with a little edge.

Avoid:

- Generic corporate sludge.
- Empty AI hype.
- Overexplaining obvious things.
- Fake urgency.
- Guaranteed-outcome claims.
- Insider jargon without context.
- Sounding like a bank compliance pamphlet that found LinkedIn.

## Visual/Verbal Feel

Preferred feel:

- Dark-luxe fintech.
- Bento-style sections.
- Command-center language.
- Sharp CTAs.
- Plain-English explanations.
- Practical operator copy.

Useful tone references:

- “Here is the signal.”
- “Here is the next move.”
- “Here is where the file may break.”
- “Here is what to clean up before applying.”
- “No application roulette.”

## Public Page Copy Rules

Public pages should answer quickly:

1. Who is this for?
2. What problem does it solve?
3. What can the user do right now?
4. What happens next?
5. What are the limits/disclaimers?

Every major public page should have:

- Clear H1.
- Short subheadline.
- Primary CTA.
- Secondary CTA where useful.
- Who-it-is-for section.
- Use-case section.
- Compliance-safe disclaimer when discussing funding outcomes.

## Partner Page Copy Rules

Partner-facing copy should emphasize:

- Better intake.
- Better routing.
- Useful tools.
- White-labeled workflows.
- Partner attribution.
- Practical enablement.
- More professional conversations with prospects.

Avoid making partner claims that imply guaranteed earnings, guaranteed commissions, or guaranteed outcomes.

Safer language:

- Built to support partner attribution.
- Designed to help partners organize and qualify opportunities.
- Helps partners have better funding conversations.
- Gives partners tools to pre-screen, educate, and route prospects.

## Tool Page Copy Rules

Tool pages should be clear and useful.

Recommended structure:

1. What this tool does.
2. Who it is for.
3. What information the user needs.
4. Tool input section.
5. Result section.
6. Suggested next steps.
7. Disclaimer.

Tool result copy should not say:

- You are approved.
- You will get this amount.
- Your rate is guaranteed.
- You qualify for this exact product.

Tool result copy should say:

- This profile may fit.
- This path may be worth reviewing.
- These are the friction points.
- These documents may be needed.
- Provider criteria apply.

## CTA Style

Preferred CTAs:

- Explore Funding Options
- Check Funding Fit
- Run a Funding Pre-Check
- Review Your Readiness
- Become a Partner
- Open the Partner Stack
- Compare Possible Paths
- Build Your Funding Plan
- Start the Intake

Avoid:

- Get Approved Now
- Guaranteed Funding
- Lock Your Rate
- Claim Your Approval
- Free Money

## Headline Style

Good headline patterns:

- Stop Guessing Where a File Fits
- Turn Funding Chaos Into a Routing System
- Check the File Before You Burn the Lead
- Funding Readiness Without Application Roulette
- Tools for Partners Who Want Cleaner Deals
- Know the Friction Before the Underwriter Does

Weak headline patterns:

- Welcome to Our Platform
- Innovative Solutions for Modern Businesses
- Empowering Growth Through Technology
- AI-Powered Synergy for Funding Success

## Microcopy Rules

Good microcopy is short, useful, and specific.

Examples:

- Takes about 2 minutes.
- No approval is guaranteed.
- Use estimates for planning only.
- Better inputs create better routing.
- Missing documents can slow review.

Avoid:

- We are thrilled to announce.
- At FundStack, we believe.
- Unlock your potential.
- Seamlessly empower your journey.

## Internal Documentation Style

Internal docs should be:

- Direct.
- Structured.
- Operational.
- Explicit about assumptions.
- Useful for future AI agents.

Use tables, checklists, and clear route/file paths where helpful.

## Compliance-Safe Disclaimer Library

Short version:

> Provider criteria apply. Approval, terms, amounts, and timing are not guaranteed.

Tool version:

> This tool is for planning and education only. It does not guarantee approval, funding, terms, rates, amounts, or timing. Actual options depend on business profile, documentation, underwriting review, and provider criteria.

Partner version:

> Partner tools support intake, education, and routing conversations. They do not replace underwriting review and should not be presented as final approval decisions.

AI output version:

> AI-generated outputs are drafts for review. Confirm accuracy, compliance, and context before sending.

## Editing Checklist

Before publishing or committing public copy, confirm:

- The page has a clear audience.
- The CTA is specific.
- Claims are compliance-safe.
- No backend provider names are exposed accidentally.
- No guaranteed approval/funding/timing language appears.
- The copy is useful, not fluffy.
- The page still sounds like FundStack, not a beige bank PDF.
