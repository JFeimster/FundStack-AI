# Compliance Rules

This document defines the public-copy and product-positioning guardrails for FundStack AI and Moonshine Capital partner assets.

FundStack AI content should help business owners and partners understand potential funding paths. It should not promise approval, guarantee terms, or imply that any tool output is a lending decision.

## Core Standard

Public-facing content must be accurate, practical, and compliance-safe.

Use this principle everywhere:

> FundStack AI helps users explore funding options based on business profile signals. Approval, terms, amounts, timing, and available products are not guaranteed and depend on provider criteria.

## Required Public Copy Guardrails

### Do Not Guarantee Approval

Avoid:

- Guaranteed approval.
- Instant approval for everyone.
- Everyone qualifies.
- No denial.
- We approve all files.

Use:

- See what options may fit your profile.
- Explore funding paths based on your business details.
- Pre-check your readiness before applying.
- Provider criteria apply.

### Do Not Guarantee Funding Amounts

Avoid:

- Get up to $500,000 guaranteed.
- You qualify for $100,000.
- Your approval amount is locked.

Use:

- Funding amounts may vary by provider, revenue, credit profile, documentation, and underwriting criteria.
- Estimated ranges are for planning only.
- Final terms depend on review and provider criteria.

### Do Not Guarantee Funding Speed

Avoid:

- Same-day funding guaranteed.
- Cash in 24 hours for everyone.
- Instant money.

Use:

- Some options may offer fast review.
- Timing depends on provider criteria, document completeness, bank connection, underwriting, and applicant profile.
- Fast funding may be available for qualified applicants.

### Do Not Give Legal, Tax, Accounting, Investment, or Lending Approval Advice

Avoid implying that FundStack AI is acting as:

- An attorney.
- CPA/accountant.
- Investment advisor.
- Licensed lender decision engine.
- Final underwriter.

Use:

- Planning support.
- Educational estimate.
- Funding readiness guidance.
- Partner-facing intake and triage support.

## Approved Language Library

### General Funding Copy

- Explore funding options based on your business profile.
- See what may fit before you submit a full application.
- Compare potential paths based on revenue, time in business, credit tier, and use case.
- Provider criteria apply.
- Approval, terms, amounts, and timing are not guaranteed.

### Tool Disclaimer

Use this or a close variant on calculators, quizzes, and pre-check tools:

> This tool is for planning and educational purposes only. It does not guarantee approval, funding, terms, rates, amounts, or timing. Actual options depend on business profile, documentation, underwriting review, and provider criteria.

### Partner-Facing Disclaimer

> Partner tools are designed to support intake, education, and routing conversations. They do not replace underwriting review and should not be presented as final approval decisions.

### AI Output Disclaimer

> AI-generated outputs are drafts for review. Confirm accuracy, compliance, and context before sending to prospects, applicants, partners, or clients.

## Backend Exposure Rules

Public pages should not expose backend lender/provider relationships, internal routing logic, private economics, or white-label operational details unless explicitly approved.

Avoid public references to:

- Backend provider names.
- Internal commission splits.
- Private routing maps.
- Underwriting thresholds that should remain operational.
- Partner bypass-protection mechanics.

Internal docs may reference routing concepts when necessary for operations.

## Partner Attribution Rules

Do not break or casually alter:

- `partner_id` parameters.
- UTM parameters.
- Referral links.
- Tally embed links.
- CRM/source-tracking links.
- Partner-specific landing pages.

When changing CTAs or forms, verify attribution first.

## Risky Claims to Review or Soften

The following claim types should be reviewed before publication:

- “0% bypass risk.”
- “#1 platform.”
- “Infinite yield.”
- “Guaranteed partner protection.”
- “Guaranteed commissions.”
- “Guaranteed same-day capital.”
- “No credit needed” without proper qualification.
- “Bad credit approved” without proper qualification.

Safer alternatives:

- Built to support partner attribution.
- Designed to reduce referral leakage.
- Built for high-performance originators.
- Fast review may be available for qualified applicants.
- Options may exist for a range of credit profiles.

## CTA Rules

Preferred CTAs:

- Explore Funding Options
- Check Funding Fit
- Run a Funding Pre-Check
- Review Your Readiness
- Become a Partner
- Open the Partner Stack
- Compare Possible Paths

Avoid CTAs that imply certainty:

- Get Approved Now
- Claim Your Guaranteed Funding
- Lock Your Approval
- Get Your Guaranteed Rate

## Review Checklist Before Publishing Public Pages

Before publishing or linking a public-facing page, confirm:

- No guaranteed approval language.
- No guaranteed amount language.
- No guaranteed timing language.
- No backend provider exposure.
- No unapproved lender/provider names.
- No final underwriting decisions implied.
- CTA points to the correct form/tool.
- Partner attribution is preserved where relevant.
- Standard disclaimer is present where a tool or estimate is shown.

## Internal vs Public Distinction

Internal docs can be more direct about routing logic, operational stages, and partner workflow.

Public pages should focus on:

- Education.
- Qualification readiness.
- General funding paths.
- Practical next steps.
- Compliance-safe conversion.

When in doubt, keep sensitive details internal and public copy white-labeled.
